Situation

On Vodafone Germany, reporting queries on large datasets were slow during high-load windows. Business users waited too long for reports, and DB load spiked during peak usage.

Task

Reduce report generation time and improve backend efficiency without changing business logic.

Action

  • Analyzed slow queries using execution plans and identified full table scans and missing indexes on reporting paths.

Result

Report generation time improved by more than 10%. Resource utilization during peak windows improved, and the team had a repeatable approach for future reporting performance issues.

Make it concrete

S: slow peak reports → T: cut time/CPU → A: EXPLAIN, composite indexes, query rewrite, validated in lower env → R: >10% faster, measured by p95 + DB CPU. Bridge: same tuning discipline scales to high-volume transactional data.
