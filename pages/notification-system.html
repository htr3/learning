Distributed Notification System

How platforms send billions of emails, SMS, push and in-app messages reliably — a Kafka-buffered pipeline with preference filtering, idempotent deduplication, multi-channel fan-out, exponential-backoff retries, priority queues, batching and a dead-letter queue for the messages that just won't deliver. Channel Trade-offs
Why one service per channel — each provider (SES, Twilio, FCM/APNs) has its own latency, cost, rate limits and failure modes. A shared pipeline (dedup, retry, DLQ, preferences) feeds channel-specific senders so you get one consistent, reliable path with per-channel tuning.
