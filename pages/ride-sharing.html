Geospatial Indexing

Spatial Index Compared

Geohash vs H3 vs QuadTree vs S2
Why Uber built H3 — geohash cells are rectangles whose neighbors are at varying distances and whose sizes jump at prefix boundaries. H3's hexagons have uniform neighbor distance (every neighbor shares an edge) and smoother size changes — ideal for the distance-based demand/supply math surge pricing needs.
