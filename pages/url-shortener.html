Capacity Estimation

A staple of the interview: size the system before designing it. Assume 100M new URLs/day and a 100:1 read:write ratio.

Why 7 characters? Base62 gives 62⁷ ≈ 3.5 trillion unique codes. At 100M/day that lasts ~95 years — 6 chars (56 billion) would run out in under 2 years.
