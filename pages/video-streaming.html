Streaming Protocols

HLS vs MPEG-DASH vs Progressive
In practice — both HLS and MPEG-DASH are adaptive, segment-based protocols driven by a manifest (playlist/MPD). HLS dominates Apple devices; DASH is codec-agnostic and open. Many platforms ship both, or use CMAF segments so one set of files serves both protocols.
