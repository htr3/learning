Frontier Ordering

BFS vs DFS vs Priority

Traversal Strategies
What real crawlers do — essentially BFS with priorities. The frontier is a priority queue keyed by importance (PageRank, freshness, host budget), not a pure FIFO or stack — so high-value pages are fetched first while breadth keeps coverage wide.
